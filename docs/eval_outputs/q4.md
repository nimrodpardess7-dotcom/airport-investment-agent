# Q4: What is the unmet flight demand in SFO airport and why?

Our estimate is that SFO is missing about **14,695 departures a year**, or **2.06M passengers**. The main reason is the runways: the two pairs of parallel runways are too close together for side-by-side landings. The biggest risk to this view is that the newest runway limit (**effective 2026-03-31**) is barely in the data yet.

**Two separate ways to size the gap (do not add them):**
- **Estimate, recovery gap:** SFO passengers are **-4.4%** vs 2019, while similar airports are at **+2.9%**. That gap works out to about **14,695 departures**, or **2.06M boardings** a year [data: T-100, 2025-05 to 2026-04] [assumption: A-24].
- **Upper bound, runway capacity:** since 2026-03-31 the FAA no longer allows side-by-side landings, even in clear weather. Clear-weather arrivals fell from **54 to 40** an hour, **14 fewer (25.9%)** [domain: SFO-02]. If every lost slot were filled **17 hours a day**, that would be **238 arrivals a day**, or **12.16M passengers a year** [assumption: A-10].

**Why the label is Airfield:** both conditions are met. **46.5%** of delays come from runways and air traffic control (**p100**, above the p75 bar), and the runways are documented as close parallels **750 ft** apart [domain: SFO-01]. There are no near misses. SFO has also been an IATA Level 2 airport since Summer 2012 because of runway capacity [domain: SFO-03].

**Another possible reason is weak demand.** For it: departures are **-10.7% (p30)** and passengers **-4.4% (p27)** vs 2019. Against it: planes are **82.6%** full (**p97**). Airlines have been flying bigger planes (seats per flight **+6.4%**), but this tool does not count that as a clear sign of up-gauging [assumption: A-09].

**What this means for investors:** airfield projects such as approach procedures and taxiways are the case. A bigger terminal won't add landings while the runway limit holds. Warning flags and approved projects weren't checked here.

**Caveats:**
- Only about **1 month** of the 12-month data reflects the new limit. The recovery-gap estimate probably doesn't capture it yet.
- The upper bound overstates the real loss, because low visibility and off-peak hours reduce it.
